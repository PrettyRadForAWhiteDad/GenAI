Is a Dutch Shepherd Right for You?

This reflection tool examines practical fit rather than appearance or enthusiasm alone. Answer every question using your current, sustainable capacity.

Please answer every question before viewing your result.
1. How much experience do you have with high-drive working dogs?
2. Can you provide structured training and engagement most days?
3. Will you provide regular mental enrichment, not only walks?
4. Are you interested in advanced training, scent work, or dog sports?
5. Does your lifestyle support an active, athletic dog?
6. Are you comfortable managing intensity, arousal, and adolescent behavior?
7. Can your environment support safe exercise, rest, and management?
8. Can you sustain training, care, costs, and contingency planning for the dog’s lifetime?

This quiz is an educational tool and is not a professional assessment of whether a particular dog is appropriate for an individual or household.
