A thinking athlete

More than energy

Purposeful movement

The standard describes a medium-sized, well-muscled, balanced dog built for endurance and efficient work.

Independent judgment

Herding heritage favored dogs able to cooperate with a handler while handling assigned tasks independently.

Responsible partnership

Success depends on compatible lifestyle, humane training, socialization, veterinary care, and a long-term commitment.
